01 · What we do

India throws away a lot of packaging. We find a use for it.

Juice cartons. Chips packets. Toothpaste tubes. Most of it is made of layers that don’t come apart, so most of it never gets recycled. It ends up in a landfill, a drain or a fire.

We worked out another way. We shred it, press it and turn it into solid boards that builders, carpenters and factories actually want to use.

We’ve been at it since 1999. We’re India’s pioneer in recycled composite boards, and we still know this material better than anyone.

02 · The process

From the bin to the building site

Four steps, one plant, no trees. Everything below is filmed on our own line.

  1. STEP 01

    Collect

    Used cartons, multi-layer plastic, flexible packaging and tubes arrive at our plant by the truckload.

  2. STEP 02

    Shred

    It’s sorted and cut down into small flakes.

  3. STEP 03

    Press

    Heat and pressure fuse the flakes into a solid sheet. No trees are cut. No formaldehyde is used.

  4. STEP 04

    Make

    The boards are cut to size and become doors, pallets, roofing, furniture and more.

06 · Comparison

Why not just use plywood?

Plywood comes from trees. MDF and particle board come from wood chips and glue, and they swell once water gets in. Our boards come from waste, and two of the three don’t mind water at all.
