What we do

Headline: India throws away a lot of packaging. We find a use for it.

Juice cartons. Chips packets. Toothpaste tubes. Most of it is made of layers that don’t come apart, so most of it never gets recycled. It ends up in a landfill, a drain or a fire.

We worked out another way. We shred it, press it and turn it into solid boards that builders, carpenters and factories actually want to use.

We’ve been at it since 1999. We’re India’s pioneer in recycled composite boards, and we still know this material better than anyone.

Button: How it works

The process

Headline: From the bin to the building site

  1. Collect. Used cartons, multi-layer plastic, flexible packaging and tubes arrive at our plant by the truckload.
  2. Shred. It’s sorted and cut down into small flakes.
  3. Press. Heat and pressure fuse the flakes into a solid sheet. No trees are cut. No formaldehyde is used. [Client to confirm]
  4. Make. The boards are cut to size and become doors, pallets, roofing, furniture and more.

Our boards

Headline: Three boards. Three kinds of waste.

  • Ecolink Boards. Water resistant. Made from used packaging, including beverage cartons. Link: Learn more
  • Ecoshakti Boards. 100% waterproof. Made from multi-layer plastic waste. Link: Visit ecoshakti.in
  • ESS Boards. 100% waterproof. Made from flexible packaging and collapsible tubes. Link: Learn more

Standard sheets are 8 x 4 feet and 4 mm to 50 mm thick. Need a different size? We’ll make it.

Our story

Deluxe Recycling (India) Pvt. Ltd. started with a plain question. What happens to a used juice carton?

The honest answer was nothing good. So we went looking for a better one.

It took years of trials, a lot of failed batches and plenty of patience. Then we had a board. Solid, strong and made entirely from packaging people had thrown out. We called it Ecolink, and we’ve been improving it ever since.

Today our plant in Palghar, Maharashtra, recycles about 3,000 tonnes of waste every month. The boards go into doors, pallets, roofing, furniture, building sites and even roads. The know-how has travelled too. Recycling plants built on our technology are working in seven countries. [Client to confirm]

Search title: Environment | How Ecolink Turns Packaging Waste Into Boards

Search description: Zero water effluent, zero solid waste, zero gas emissions. See how Ecolink keeps packaging waste out of landfills and turns it into boards.

Hero

Headline: A juice carton is used for ten minutes. Then what?

Subhead: We’ve spent 27 years answering that.

The problem

Packaging is made to be used once and thrown away. And a lot of it is hard to recycle, because it’s layers of paper, plastic and foil stuck together. The usual recyclers can’t pull them apart. So it gets dumped, burnt or left to pile up.

What we do about it

We take it. All of it. We shred it and press it into boards, and the boards become things people use every day.

What we take in: Aseptic beverage cartons · Multi-layer plastic · Poly Al waste · Mix plastic waste · Flexible packaging · Collapsible tubes · Mill plastic waste

What it adds up to: About 3,000 tonnes a month. 37,000 tonnes a year.

Three zeros

  • Zero water effluent. Nothing dirty goes down the drain.
  • Zero solid waste. Everything that comes in goes out as product.
  • Zero gas emissions. The process doesn’t add to the air.

Safe to make. Safe to use.

Our boards are made without formaldehyde. [Client to confirm]
