Our process

From waste to board, in four steps.

  1. 01

    Buy waste

    Plastic, wood, paper, tyres, cotton and other hard-to-recycle waste is collected from suppliers across India.

  2. 02

    Shred & sort

    Feedstock is shredded, screened and graded so every input stream is consistent and clean.

  3. 03

    Composite

    Materials are combined and pressed under heat into dense, uniform boards and sheets.

  4. 04

    Finish & dispatch

    Boards are trimmed, surfaced, quality-checked and shipped to customers in India and abroad.
