01 · What it is

Every Ecolink board starts as something someone used once. A juice carton. A milk pack. A wrapper. We shred it, press it and end up with a flat, solid sheet that works the way a board should.

No tree is cut to make it.

Water Water resistant Resistant100% waterproof
Good to know

Ecolink is water resistant, not waterproof. It handles damp and splashes well. If your board will sit in standing water, look at Ecoshakti or ESS instead.
