This website is operated by Deluxe Recycling (India) Pvt. Ltd. ("Ecolink India", "we", "us"). Questions about this policy can be sent to info@ecolinkindia.com.

2. What we collect

When you submit an enquiry form we collect the details you provide: name, email address, phone number (optional), enquiry type and message content. We do not ask for payment details through this website.

3. Why we collect it

To respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations, fulfil contractual obligations and maintain records required by law. The lawful basis is your consent (given when you submit the form) and our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies to make the site work. Non-essential analytics cookies are only set if you accept them via the cookie banner; if you decline, no analytics are loaded. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate this website and respond to enquiries, and where required by law.

Enquiry records are kept for as long as needed to handle your request and for statutory retention periods, then deleted.

You may request access to the personal data we hold about you, ask for corrections, or request deletion, by writing to info@ecolinkindia.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
