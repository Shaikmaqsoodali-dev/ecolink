01 · The idea

You bring the waste and the land.

You bring the waste and the land. We bring 27 years of knowing how to turn it into boards.

We didn’t learn this from a textbook. We learned it by running our own plant since 1999. So when we build one for you, we’re handing over something that already works.

02 · Eco-friendly technology

No washing. Really.

Most plastic recycling starts with a wash. That means water, drains and dirty effluent to deal with. Our process skips it. Less water, less cost, less mess.

06 · Turnkey, start to finish

How we work with you

  1. Plan

    We design the plant layout and process for your waste and your space.

  2. Build

    The machinery is made and supplied.

  3. Install

    We set it up on site.

  4. Calibrate

    Trial runs until the boards come out right.

  5. Train

    Your team learns to run it.

  6. Support

    We stay on the line when you need us, including help with EPR planning.

EPR,
in plain words.

Brands and producers are now responsible for the packaging they put into the market. Recycling is how they meet that responsibility. A plant like this helps you do it.
